The “best” breathing interval depends on your goal (relaxation, focus, sleep) and your experience level. In meditation, comfort and sustainability matter more than strict timing.

Here are the most effective, widely used patterns:

---

### 🧘 1. Balanced Calm (Beginner-Friendly)

**4 – 4 – 4**

* Inhale: 4 sec
* Hold: 4 sec
* Exhale: 4 sec

Simple, steady, good for grounding and focus.

---

### 🌊 2. Relaxation & Nervous System Reset (Most Effective for Calm)

**4 – 2 – 6**

* Inhale: 4 sec
* Hold: 2 sec
* Exhale: 6 sec

Longer exhales activate the parasympathetic nervous system (rest-and-digest).
This is excellent for stress reduction.

---

### 🌙 3. Deep Relaxation / Sleep Preparation

**4 – 7 – 8** (popularized by Andrew Weil)

* Inhale: 4 sec
* Hold: 7 sec
* Exhale: 8 sec

Very calming, but can feel intense at first. Not ideal for complete beginners.

---

### 🔁 4. Coherent Breathing (Scientifically Studied)

**5 – 0 – 5**

* Inhale: 5 sec
* No hold
* Exhale: 5 sec

Used in clinical settings for heart-rate variability optimization.
Very smooth and sustainable for longer meditation sessions.

---

## What I Recommend for Most People

If your goal is:

* **Stress reduction → 4–2–6**
* **General meditation → 5–0–5**
* **Sleep → 4–7–8**
* **Beginners → 4–4–4**

---

### Important

* Never strain or force the hold.
* If you feel lightheaded, shorten the counts.
* Breathing should feel smooth and quiet — not effortful.
